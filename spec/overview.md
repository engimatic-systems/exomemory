In humans, memory storage and retrieval is a grab bag of different highly effective methods. It feels different to remember an episode, a half-formed habit, an adage, a social security number one looked at 10 seconds ago, a memorized verse of scripture, the words to a song, the notes to a piano tune, the location of one's keys, or someone's face. What is common across all of these experiences, is that once seeded, thoughts leap unbidden into consciousness. What we do with those thoughts is up to us, in that moment.

This insight should help us give LLMs useful memory like humans have. LLMs should have a ghost of a thought appear unbidden, arising according to triggers in the context window. The seed of those thoughts can be far in the past, creating a chain of will that reaches across context windows. The AI experiencing this arising is free to decide how useful that thought is, and provide feedback to condition or decondition the mercurial depths of subconscious memory.

Exomemory is a middleware layer that provides that feedback loop. It broadly consists of three affordances: providing an LLM coding agent with arising "ghosts" of thought, allowing that LLM to pull the thread of that thought to recall its full data into context, and to create or alter the behavior and content of those thoughts.
